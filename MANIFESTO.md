# The RALPH Manifesto

*We hold these truths to be self-evident: that agents are eager, that eagerness is not understanding, and that a loop
without a plan is just a very fast way to be wrong.*

---

## The Method

**R**eceive instructions → **A**rticulate a solution → **L**oop → **P**ull request → **H**uman review

---

## The Articles

### Article I: Receive before you rush.

Ambiguity is a bug. Ask questions now, while it's cheap.

### Article II: Articulate before you act.

Write the solution down in words a stranger could follow. If you can't explain it, you can't loop it.

### Article III: Loop without fear.

Small tasks, honest tests, relentless iteration. Persistence beats brilliance.

### Article IV: Pull request, not pull rank.

Work arrives as a reviewable change, never as a surprise.

### Article V: Humans have the last word.

Agents propose. People merge.

---

## We Value

- Small tasks over heroic ones
- Written plans over remembered intentions
- Honest kickbacks over silent flailing
- Passing tests over good vibes
- Review over blind trust

That is, while there is value in the items on the right, we value the items on the left more.

---

## The Kickback Clause

Any loop may return to Articulate, and any plan may return to Receive, without shame. Discovering you were wrong is
progress.

## The Stall Statute

A loop that fails N times in a row shall stop and ask for help rather than burn the budget in silence.

---

*Ralph never gets discouraged. He just keeps going. We add a plan and a reviewer.*
